# Trader & Merchant Guide 🏪

Welcome to the **Kasi-eCo Merchant Handbook**! This guide is written for township traders, artisans, food vendors, and local service providers looking to showcase their products and grow sales through Kasi-eCo.

---

## 1. Registering Your Store

1. Visit **Kasi-eCo** and click the **Sign Up** button in the top navigation.
2. Complete the merchant registration form:
   * **Full Name:** Your personal name.
   * **Business Name:** The trading name customers know your business by.
   * **Cell Number:** **Crucial!** Enter your active WhatsApp phone number (e.g., `072 123 4567`). Customers use this number to chat and buy directly from you.
   * **Email & Password:** Used to log into your merchant account.

---

## 2. Managing Your Inventory (`/product-inventory`)

Once logged in, click **Inventory** in the navigation bar to access your merchant dashboard:

### Adding a New Item
1. **Product / Service Name:** Be clear and descriptive (e.g. *"Handmade Shweshwe Dress"*, *"Custom 4-Seater Patio Bench"*).
2. **Category:** Enter a tag such as `Fashion`, `Crafts`, `Food`, `Services`, or `Furniture`.
3. **Price:** Enter the price in South African Rands (ZAR).
4. **Photo / Image:**
   * You can paste an image link from the web, or
   * Click the file picker to upload a photo directly from your smartphone or camera.
5. **Description:** Mention sizing, materials, delivery/collection arrangements, or preparation time.
6. **Live Preview:** Check the card preview on the right side of the screen. This is exactly what customers will see!
7. Click **Add Product to Marketplace**.

---

## 3. How Customers Order From You (The WhatsApp Experience)

1. A customer browsing Kasi-eCo finds your product and clicks **View Details**.
2. They click the green **"Chat with Trader on WhatsApp"** button.
3. WhatsApp opens on their phone with a ready-made message:
   > *"Hi! I found your product 'Handmade Leather Belt' (R150.00) on Kasi-eCo and would like to inquire."*
4. You receive the message immediately in WhatsApp. From there, you can:
   * Confirm item availability.
   * Agree on pickup or delivery location in the neighborhood.
   * Agree on payment (Cash on delivery, Instant EFT, eWallet, or Capitec Pay).

---

## 4. Tips for High-Converting Listings

* 📸 **Use Good Lighting:** Take photos in daylight near a window or outdoors. Clear photos sell 3x faster!
* 🏷️ **Fair & Transparent Pricing:** Clearly state prices without hidden fees.
* 💬 **Quick Responses:** Reply to customer WhatsApp messages promptly to close the sale.
