The Solution

Introducing SwiftMatch AI

A two-sided AI platform that connects verified township SMEs to corporate procurement opportunities — at scale, over WhatsApp.

🔍
AI Document Extraction & Verified Profiles

Vision-LLMs extract CIPC, SARS Tax PIN, B-BBEE affidavits and trade references from smartphone photos into tamper-proof capability profiles.

🎯
Semantic Matching Engine

1536-dimensional vector embeddings match corporate RFPs to ranked SME shortlists with transparent, auditable score breakdowns.

💬
WhatsApp-Native Bidding Copilot

94–96% of SA internet users are on WhatsApp. Matched SMEs receive an AI-generated bid alert and guided quotation assistant — zero portal login required.

💰
ESD Capital Routing

Instantly matches shortlisted SMEs against NEF, SEFA, and corporate ESD grant eligibility — addressing South Africa's R350B SME funding gap.

How It Works

End-to-end in 5 steps.

1
SME Onboards via WhatsApp

Photos compliance docs. Vision-LLM builds verified profile.

2
Corporate Submits RFP

PDF upload or plain-language brief. AI extracts all criteria.

3
Hybrid Matching Engine Runs

Hard filters + semantic vector similarity + capacity scoring.

4
Ranked Shortlist + Alerts

Corporate sees match cards. SME gets WhatsApp bid alert & quote wizard.

5
Funding & Dashboard

ESD grant matching. Real-time B-BBEE impact reporting for corporates.

🛡️ Human-in-the-loop always. AI generates transparent shortlists. Corporate procurement officers make the final call. SMEs retain full right of response.
